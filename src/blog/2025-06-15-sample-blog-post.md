## What is PSA Technology?

Pressure Swing Adsorption (PSA) is a cutting-edge technology used to purify raw biogas into high-quality Compressed Biogas (CBG). This process separates methane from carbon dioxide and other impurities, achieving methane purity levels of over 95%.

## How PSA Works

The PSA process operates on the principle that different gases are adsorbed onto solid surfaces at different pressures. The system uses specialized adsorbent materials that selectively capture CO₂, H₂S, and other impurities while allowing methane to pass through.

## Key Components of PSA System

- **Adsorber Vessels:** Contain the adsorbent material
- **Compressor:** Increases gas pressure for adsorption
- **Valve System:** Controls gas flow between vessels
- **Control System:** Automates the adsorption cycles
- **Product Buffer Tank:** Stores purified CBG

## Advantages of PSA Technology

- High methane purity (95-99%)
- Low operating costs
- Fully automated operation
- Compact footprint
- Environmentally friendly process

## Applications

The high-purity CBG produced through PSA technology can be used in various applications including automotive fuel, industrial heating, power generation, and as a feedstock for chemical production.
